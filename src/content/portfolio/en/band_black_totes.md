We've got a house band at Picnic, called 'The Black Totes' (named after the black boxes we transport our frozen articles), that performs at internal parties and the yearly ski trip. Mostly hits and covers: think of I Will Survive, Murder On The Dancefloor and an ABBA-medley. I play keys!
